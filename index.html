01 — About

Systems engineering over slideware

I'm a Software Engineering student (Batch 2023, graduating June 2027) at Yangzhou University, in the College of Information and Artificial Intelligence (College of Industrial Software). My work sits where pretrained models meet real deliverables: I wire speech recognition, machine translation, text-to-speech and media processing into pipelines that produce finished output files.

Everything I publish is honest about its scope. These are applied systems that orchestrate pretrained models — there is no model training and no novel algorithm in the pipeline work. What there is: deliberate constraint engineering (a model chosen to fit a 6 GB GPU rather than by default), reliability work (staged, resumable execution), quality control for machine-generated text, and measurement — a re-runnable evaluation harness rather than an assumption that it works.

Since October 2026 my research has moved closer to the models themselves: a knowledge distillation architecture for ECG arrhythmia detection under Dr. Zhijun Xiao, currently at literature review, problem formulation and experimental planning.

Independent · Open source · Apache-2.0

AI Video Dubbing Pipeline

Python · Faster-Whisper · PyTorch · LLMs · Text-to-Speech · FFmpeg

End-to-end automatic video dubbing in ten stages: FFmpeg audio extraction → word-level speech recognition (Faster-Whisper) → subtitle segmentation → context-aware LLM translation → duration optimisation (rewriting each line to fit its original time window) → voice generation with optional cloning (Fish Speech / CosyVoice2 / edge-tts) → timing adjustment (≤ ±5% tempo) → EBU-R128-style loudness matching → mux, keeping the original audio as a second track.

Switchable cloud/local LLM backends, selected by configuration to fit a 6 GB GPU; staged, resumable execution so any stage range can be re-run after a failure.

Product-Video Localization and Technical Translation Tooling

Python · LLMs · Audio Processing · QA Automation

Customer-facing product-video localization for an industrial water-analysis instrumentation client. Machine translation alone isn't acceptable for customer material, so this variant adds human-in-the-loop review gates, adversarial QA passes that actively hunt for mistranslation, invented specification and terminology drift, a persistent translation memory keeping a term identical across every video and document, and a tight-pacing export cut for shorter delivery durations.

~3,900 LOC. Delivered dubbed product videos (normal and tight-pacing) ship in the repository. In production use at Pyxis Lab, where it cut localization turnaround from a full day to about 20 minutes.

Python · LLMs · NLP · Document Layout Engine

Tooling to translate a large industrial product catalogue between English and Simplified Chinese while preserving layout in place — redacting text and re-inserting it at its original coordinates, colours and weights — with product codes, brand names and units deliberately left untranslated. Comic2Video: Manga-to-Narrated-Video Engine

Python · Vision-Language Models · OCR · DAG Scheduler · pytest

An end-to-end multimodal pipeline that turns raw manga pages into finished narrated video: panel detection, VLM page understanding, script generation, text-to-speech and FFmpeg video assembly — every stage resumable, every artifact reproducible.

The interesting part is the dynamic DAG scheduler with an AI-routing layer: it measures each page and automatically escalates the hard ones from a lightweight model to a larger one, so a whole chapter renders inside a 6 GB consumer GPU instead of falling over. Correctness is pinned down by a 17-module pytest suite.

21 complete chapter videos rendered (4–9 min each, H.264/AAC) — the pipeline's own output, not a demo reel.
