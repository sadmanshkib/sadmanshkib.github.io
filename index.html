01 — About

Systems engineering over slideware

I'm a Software Engineering student (Batch 2023, graduating June 2027) at Yangzhou University, in the School of Information Engineering — the School of Artificial Intelligence. My work sits where pretrained models meet real deliverables: I wire speech recognition, machine translation, text-to-speech and media processing into pipelines that produce finished output files.

Everything I publish is honest about its scope. These are applied systems that orchestrate pretrained models — there is no model training and no novel algorithm here. What there is: deliberate constraint engineering (a model chosen to fit a 6 GB GPU rather than by default), reliability work (staged, resumable execution), quality control for machine-generated text, and measurement — a re-runnable evaluation harness rather than an assumption that it works.

Independent · Open source · Apache-2.0

AI Video Dubbing Pipeline

End-to-end automatic video dubbing in ten stages: FFmpeg audio extraction → word-level speech recognition (Faster-Whisper) → subtitle segmentation → context-aware LLM translation → duration optimisation (rewriting each line to fit its original time window) → voice generation with optional cloning (Fish Speech / CosyVoice2 / edge-tts) → timing adjustment (≤ ±5% tempo) → EBU-R128-style loudness matching → mux, keeping the original audio as a second track.

Switchable cloud/local LLM backends, selected by configuration to fit a 6 GB GPU; staged, resumable execution so any stage range can be re-run after a failure.

Product-Video Localisation, EN → Simplified Chinese

Customer-facing product-video localisation for an industrial water-analysis instrumentation client. Machine translation alone isn't acceptable for customer material, so this variant adds human-in-the-loop review gates, adversarial QA passes that actively hunt for mistranslation, invented specification and terminology drift, a persistent translation memory keeping a term identical across every video and document, and a tight-pacing export cut for shorter delivery durations.

~3,900 LOC. Delivered dubbed product videos (normal and tight-pacing) ship in the repository.

Translation Tooling with Persistent Term Memory

Tooling to translate a large industrial product catalogue EN → Simplified Chinese while preserving layout in place — redacting text and re-inserting it at its original coordinates, colours and weights — with product codes, brand names and units deliberately left untranslated. Scripted extraction and QA helpers, a persistent EN→ZH term memory, and the whole workflow packaged as a reusable agent skill.
